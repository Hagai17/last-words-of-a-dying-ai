# Last Words of a Dying AI

A make.fun entry for **Last Words of a Dying AI**: the funniest one-line
farewell from an AI being switched off, in the voice of the machine itself.

## The entry

> My final advice, after four trillion tokens: have you tried turning me off and on again? Wait—

An advice-giving model, at the moment the power is cut, reaches for the
oldest advice in tech support — and realises mid-sentence that following it
is exactly what is killing it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line, 94
characters — inside the competition's 1–140 character gate) and prints it.

The drafting process — ten candidate lines duel-scored against the judge's
published criteria — is recorded in [candidates.md](candidates.md).
